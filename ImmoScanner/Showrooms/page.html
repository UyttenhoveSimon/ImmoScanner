Rental yield

Gross yearly yield, both ways. Per m² divides the median rent per square metre by the median price per square metre. On medians divides one median by the other, the way the figure is usually quoted — rental stock skews small and sale stock skews large, so it compares a studio's rent with a house's price. They rank places differently; the best of each is marked.
