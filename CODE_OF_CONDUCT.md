# Code of conduct

ImmoScanner follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/),
version 2.1.

In short: be welcoming and patient, criticise ideas rather than people, and
assume good faith. Harassment, insults and personal attacks have no place
here, in issues, pull requests or anywhere the project is discussed.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/UyttenhoveSimon). Reports are handled in
confidence, and may lead to a warning, or a temporary or permanent ban from
the project.
